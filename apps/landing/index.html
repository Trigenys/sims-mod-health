LESS GUESSING, MORE EVIDENCE

A health view for a folder that usually becomes chaos.

Sims Mod Health turns filenames, package metadata, script archives, registry evidence, and exception reports into one readable picture.

01

Scan locally

Inventory your Mods folder without sending raw .package or .ts4script files to a server.

mc_cmd_center.ts4script Healthy relationship_overhaul.package Unknown cas_eyelashes.package Duplicate
02

Spot duplicate and conflict risk

Exact hashes and DBPF resource overlap are kept separate, so a possible conflict is not mislabeled as a guaranteed break.

03

Read exception clues

Parse lastException, lastUIException, MCCC, and Better Exceptions reports into evidence-linked candidates — not blame.

04

Update with a way back

Supported updates create a restore point, stage and verify the replacement, check dependencies, and keep rollback evidence.

05

Unknown stays unknown

Missing or stale source evidence never becomes “Broken” just because a website or API is unavailable.

BUILT AROUND YOUR FOLDER, NOT YOUR DATA

Your Mods folder is not an analytics feed.

Core scanning and inspection happen on your machine. Registry lookups use the minimum metadata needed to identify a mod and resolve health evidence.

Beta 1

Useful now. Deliberately not pretending to be finished.

The first public build is Windows-only and the MSI is not yet code-signed. Some registry-backed features need a reachable Registry API. Bulk updates and multi-file archive updates are intentionally disabled.
